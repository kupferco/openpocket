How it works

  1. You talk. Use Claude or another assistant on your phone to agree on a change.
  2. It codes. OpenPocket runs the work in the cloud on your own repo. No laptop needed.
  3. You preview. Every change gets a link you can open and try.
  4. You ship. Say the word and it moves to staging, then production.

Early days: OpenPocket is under active development and parts of this are still planned.

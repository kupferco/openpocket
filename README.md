# OpenPocket

Build and ship your apps from your phone, by voice. Think "Lovable without a keyboard".

> **Early days.** OpenPocket is new and under active development. Nothing described below is built yet unless it says otherwise; this is the plan.

## The idea

1. You talk to Claude (or another assistant) on your phone.
2. Together you agree on a change to your app.
3. OpenPocket runs the coding in the cloud, on your own GitHub repo. *(planned)*
4. You get a preview link to try the result. *(planned)*
5. When you say so, OpenPocket deploys it. *(planned)*

Your code stays in your own repo, and no laptop is needed.

## Status

Just getting started. Everything above is planned.
